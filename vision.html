05/06Chapter Five — The Horizon

Vision

Indian Quality, Recognised Everywhere.

Our vision is to transform Indreva into an internationally recognised trading and manufacturing company — establishing our own manufacturing units within three to five years, expanding into import and export, building a strong international supply network, and taking Indian-quality products to global markets.

The growth path: sourcing, distribution, manufacturing, international trade

Stage 01

Sourcing

Earning trust as a dependable source of quality-critical raw materials — the foundation every later stage is built on.

Stage 02

Distribution

Expanding reach across India, becoming the default sourcing relationship for more manufacturers, in more categories.

Stage 03

Manufacturing

Establishing Indreva's own manufacturing units within three to five years — controlling quality at the source.

Stage 04

International Trade

Import, export, and a strong international supply network — taking Indian-quality products to global markets.

This is a staged plan, built on proof at each step — not a single leap. Every stage compounds into the reach and standing of an internationally recognised group.
