About Indreva

Built By Two Founders Who Wanted Trading Done Differently

Indreva Trade Group was founded by Mohit and Sahil, who shared a simple conviction: Indian manufacturers deserve a sourcing partner that is as reliable as it is easy to work with. What started as a plan to connect trusted suppliers with serious buyers across India has grown into a company built to operate at the standard of the industry's largest players — without losing the responsiveness of a founder-led team.

Indreva began with high-value raw materials across pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, food, and agro sectors, deliberately choosing categories where quality verification and supplier accountability matter most. The plan from day one has been staged growth — from trusted sourcing partner, to distributor, to manufacturer — each stage building on proof, not promises.

Indra + Eva

The name Indreva combines Indra — the King — with Eva — Always. Together: Always the King. A standard the company holds itself to in every consignment, not a claim it makes lightly.
