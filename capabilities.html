03/06Chapter Three — The Capability

Capabilities

Four Disciplines. One Standard.

01

Capability 01

Sourcing & Supplier Vetting

Every supplier relationship starts with verification, not a catalogue. Indreva identifies and qualifies reliable manufacturers before a rupee moves — checking capacity, consistency and track record so buyers never inherit a supplier's risk.

  • Multi-point supplier qualification before onboarding
  • Ongoing performance review, not a one-time check
  • Direct relationships — no unverified middlemen in the chain
02

Capability 02

Quality Assurance

Quality is verified, not assumed. Every consignment is checked and documented at every stage of the sourcing process — so what arrives matches what was promised, consistently, order after order.

  • Stage-wise verification from supplier to dispatch
  • Documentation issued with every batch, as standard
  • Corrective action on any deviation, before it reaches you
03

Capability 03

Logistics & Delivery

Manufacturing schedules don't wait, and neither does Indreva's supply chain. Dependable delivery across India, on schedule, is treated as a core capability — not an afterthought outsourced without oversight.

  • Pan-India delivery network with tracked consignments
  • Schedule-first planning around manufacturing timelines
  • Proactive communication on any delay, before you have to ask
04

Capability 04

Compliance & Documentation

Complete paperwork with every consignment — sourcing records, quality documentation and statutory compliance, filed correctly the first time. It is the quiet discipline that makes everything else auditable.

  • Complete documentation issued with every shipment
  • Statutory and regulatory compliance built into process
  • Full traceability from supplier to delivery
